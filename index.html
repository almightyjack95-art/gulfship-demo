GulfShip · Demo screencast

Embedded Shopify admin app. Recorded on a development store with fictional data (2 min 6 s).

What the screencast shows:

  1. Dashboard: shipments on the way, shipments that need attention, and delivery stats for the last 30 days.
  2. Orders: order #1003 is ready to ship, and a shipment is booked with the manual carrier (Aramex as the display name, tracking number, weight and parcel size).
  3. Shipment page: the tracking timeline, plus a tracking update added (in transit, picked up at the Kuwait City hub).
  4. Needs Attention: the queue of shipments with delivery exceptions.
  5. COD settlements: upload a courier remittance CSV to match cash-on-delivery payouts with shipments.
  6. Carriers: the manual carrier, plus Aramex, DHL, FedEx and UPS connection settings.
  7. Billing: the active plan, monthly shipment usage and plan changes through Shopify.
